# Logos

`placeholder-logo.png` is a temporary logo supplied by the store owner, used until the final Amrit Herbals mark exists. It shows the wordmark "G-Feno Cleanz" in black with a green leaf.

Use it on `surface-100` or `surface-200` only, at 40–64px tall, with at least `space-4` clear space. Do not recolour or stretch it. Replace it with an SVG and a transparent PNG once the final logo is ready.
